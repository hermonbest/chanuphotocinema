## Brand & Style

**CHANU PHOTO CINEMA** is a premier cinematography and photography atelier directed by Chanuiel, operating across Addis Ababa, Haramaya, and destinations worldwide. The studio's work spans four core pillars:
1. **Live Concerts & Stadium Music Festivals**: High-energy multi-camera cinematography, stage lighting design, and live performance documentation.
2. **Fine-Art Weddings & 2.39:1 Widescreen Cinema**: Emotional, heirloom wedding films and natural outdoor couple portraiture.
3. **University Graduations & Academic Milestones**: Prestigious academic folios, custom-embroidered sashes (e.g., Haramaya University Class of 2026), and celebratory family portraits.
4. **Editorial Fashion & Natural Light Garden Portraiture**: Outdoor lifestyle, floral detail studies, and fine jewelry photography.

## Colors

- **Canvas & Surfaces**: `#FAF9F6` warm alabaster base, `#FFFFFF` crisp card surfaces, and `#F8F6F1` warm stone containers.
- **High-Contrast Typography**: `#141416` deep obsidian headings (16:1 contrast) and `#38373D` deep slate body copy (> 8.5:1 contrast).
- **Accents**: `#996515` antique bronze for links/subtitles (> 4.5:1 contrast) and `#D4AF37` champagne gold for prominent CTA buttons.
- **Protective Scrims**: All photo and video cards utilize dark gradient backing (`from-black/95 via-black/50 to-transparent`) ensuring 100% legibility over any live concert or bright outdoor scene.

## Typography

- **Headlines**: **Cormorant Garamond** (500/600 weight) for timeless cinematic authority.
- **Body & UI**: **Plus Jakarta Sans** (400, 500, 600 weight) for razor-sharp clarity and accessible form controls.
