The Vision

From electric concert stages to intimate heirloom vows.

Founded under the directorial eye of Chanuiel, CHANU PHOTO CINEMA captures the full spectrum of human celebration. Whether directing dynamic multi-camera live sound cinema for packed stadium concerts or filming romantic outdoor weddings in natural golden light, we treat every frame as an archival masterwork.

From prestigious academic milestones like Haramaya University graduation ceremonies to delicate outdoor garden portraiture and Ethiopian cultural heritage, our studio delivers uncompromising visual fidelity, rich tonal color, and unforgettable cinematic impact.
